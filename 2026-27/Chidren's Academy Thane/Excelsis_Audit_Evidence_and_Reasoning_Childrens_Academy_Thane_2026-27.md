## 2. Scoring Methodology & Calibration Rationale

The scoring across the 60 indicators in the CSV is calibrated on the standard Excelsis 1–5 scale based on objective physical evidence:
- **Score 5 (Outstanding / Exemplary):** Verified benchmark practices exceeding standard compliance (e.g., student civic action, AI remedial workflows, verified lab testing).
- **Score 4 (Good / Commendable):** Verified statutory compliance with positive initiatives, thorough registers, and zero identified defects.
- **Score 3 (Satisfactory / Baseline Compliant):** Standard operational compliance confirmed through routine checklist checks, or neutral baseline score for uninspected parameters.
- **Score 2 (Needs Improvement / Action Required):** Direct physical gaps, safety/privacy deficiencies, or incomplete administrative procedures identified during inspection.
- **Score 1 (Critical Deficiency):** Immediate severe hazard or statutory non-compliance (none identified during this audit).
