## 2. Scoring Methodology & Calibration Rationale

Scoring across all 60 indicators is calibrated strictly against Excelsis assessment standards using a conservative 2–4 rating scale:

- **Score 4 (Good / Commendable):** Verified areas of excellence, commendable initiatives, active student participation, exemplary administrative documentation, or aesthetic quality with zero defects (e.g., active library programs, well-maintained co-curricular files, CCTV review rigor, canteen staff compliance, aesthetic music rooms).
- **Score 3 (Satisfactory / Baseline Compliant):** Routine statutory compliance confirmed through checklist inspection with zero identified gaps, or standard neutral baseline score for parameters operating normally without explicit defects.
- **Score 2 (Needs Improvement / Action Required):** Any parameter where a direct physical defect, chemical safety hazard, procedural gap, missing register, pending statutory clearance (PCC/medical), or administrative deficiency was identified during the audit inspection.
- **Score 1 (Critical Hazard):** Uncontrolled life-safety threat (not applied; all identified gaps are actively manageable and addressed via remedial action plans).
